Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026

01. Introduction

Behind The Scenes ("BTS", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring complete transparency regarding how your data is collected, processed, and protected. We operate an authentic dating and cultural connection platform built on real connections with zero tolerance for catfishing or fake profiles.

02. Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to deliver a secure, authentic, and verified dating experience:

  • Account Identification: Legal full name, email address, age/birth year verification, and heritage/country of roots (Ghana, Mauritius, Botswana, Namibia, Morocco).
  • Authentic Profile Content: Unedited candid photos, raw voice introduction recordings, and authentic lifestyle prompts.
  • Biometric Anti-Catfish Face Check & Verified Photo: A live front-camera selfie is analyzed on your device using a facial cascade detector to confirm authentic human presence (rejecting screens, objects, and pets). Once verified, this live photo is set as your verified main profile picture and stored securely in our cloud storage so matches can trust your authenticity. Biometric facial analysis is performed on-device and raw biometric vectors are never sold or transferred.
  • OAuth 2.0 Credentials: When opting to sign in via Google or Facebook (Meta), we securely receive your verified name, email address, and profile photo.

03. How Your Data is Used

  • Authentic Matching: Delivering real-time card recommendations, opposite-gender filters, age preferences, and heritage discovery.
  • Interactive Real-Time Chat & Presence: Syncing live chat messages, voice notes, and live online/offline presence rooms.
  • Community Safety: Enforcing strict 18+ age restrictions, moderation against objectionable content, and immediate automated spam suppression.

04. Data Retention & Permanent Deletion

You retain 100% ownership over your personal data. You may permanently delete your profile, messages, matches, swipes, and uploaded media at any moment directly within the app.

⚡ Instant In-App Data Purge

Navigate to Profile → Delete Account (Data Purge). Your data will be immediately and irreversibly purged from our Supabase PostgreSQL servers. Alternatively, visit our User Data Deletion Portal.
