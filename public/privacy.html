Privacy Policy for Behind The Scenes

Effective Date: October 3, 2026

Behind The Scenes ("BTS", "we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how your data is collected, processed, and protected.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Full name, email address, age/birth year, and country/roots.
  • Profile & Media: Candid profile photos, unedited voice intro notes, and lifestyle prompts.
  • Biometric Verification Data: On-device facial verification selfies to ensure anti-catfish authenticity. Face embeddings/selfies are processed securely and not sold to third parties.
  • OAuth Information: When signing in with Google or Facebook (Meta), we receive basic identity information (name, email, profile picture) authorized by you.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate your identity and provide matchmaking and real-time chat services.
  • To verify authenticity and eliminate bots and catfishing profiles.
  • To display your public profile and cultural heritage details to prospective matches within your preference filters.

3. Data Deletion & User Rights

You have full ownership of your data. You can delete your account and all associated messages, matches, swipes, and photos at any time in the app under Settings → Delete Account, or by visiting our User Data Deletion page.
