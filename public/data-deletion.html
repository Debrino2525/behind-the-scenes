User Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with Meta Platform Rules, Google Play Data Safety, and Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v), Behind The Scenes provides a clear and permanent data deletion mechanism for all registered users.

Method 1: Instant In-App Data Purge (Recommended)

Step 1: Open the Behind The Scenes mobile app.
Step 2: Tap on the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
Step 3: Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account (Data Purge).
Step 4: Confirm deletion. Your profile, photos, voice notes, matches, messages, and swipes will be permanently purged from our database immediately.

Method 2: Email Data Deletion Request

If you have deleted the app or cannot access your account, you may request permanent deletion of your data by emailing us:

  • Support Email: richard.k.appiah@outlook.com
  • Subject Line: Data Deletion Request - Behind The Scenes
  • Required Info: Include the email address associated with your BTS account.

Requests will be processed and completely purged from all servers within 48 hours.
